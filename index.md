# Privacy Policy for I Promise I Can Fly

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game *I Promise I Can Fly*.

## Data Collection and Use
This app uses Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded ads. AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data to personalize and measure ads.

No personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account data is collected or stored by the developer.

## Data Sharing
AdMob may share limited data (such as device identifiers and coarse location) with its advertising partners for the purpose of delivering and measuring ads.

The developer does not share any data with third parties.

## Data Security
All data processed by AdMob is encrypted in transit.

## Data Deletion
The developer does not collect or store personal data and therefore cannot provide a data deletion mechanism. Users may manage their Google advertising settings at:
https://www.google.com/settings/ads
